Hamlet / original construction board

Bean’s little home

A sunny, calm cutaway built around things that can be used. It borrows no artwork: the room on this board and in the study is original vector geometry.

Palette
cream / deep forest / rust / honey / muted sage

Soft afternoon light with enough dark ink to make each small object feel touchable.

Construction rules
  1. Keep open floor in the centre for the bean’s walk.
  2. Group objects by their purpose, like small corners of a real home.
  3. Use familiar material cues: wood grain, cork, paper, ceramic, woven fabric.
  4. Reserve labels for feedback after an interaction.
Public primary references
  • Unpacking / Witch Beam — domestic storytelling through thoughtfully placed things.
  • A Little to the Left / Max Inferno — tactile, legible household interaction.
  • Spiritfarer / Thunder Lotus — hand-crafted warmth and care-centred space.
