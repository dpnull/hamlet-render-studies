Paper street elevation

A proposed alternate town language: modular architectural facades, small interior cutaways and a lived-in street, drawn as shallow painted paper rather than a pixel cutaway or a generic product interface.

Drawing rules

Read in layersSilhouette, room band, opening, one activity, then ornament. Clear before cute.
Shallow paperOne front face, one small return and one lower-right shadow. Elevation first, not fake isometric.
Rooms as sentencesOne task and up to three props per open room. Let closed facades create breathing space.
Street as a systemA continuous curb, pavement and lane hold movable parcels together. Plants live at seams.

Primary visual references

  1. Kairosoft — Mega Mall Story: room-band hierarchy and compact activity in section.
  2. Townscaper — Oskar Stålberg / Raw Fury: a small architectural grammar varied across a growing town.
  3. Zupagrafika — Brutal East II: paper construction expressed through folds, returns and shadows.
  4. MVRDV — (w)Ego: cutaway rooms connected by circulation and calibrated with people.

Observations and concrete rules are recorded in research.json. This board is a proposal for review, not a selected style or production art.
