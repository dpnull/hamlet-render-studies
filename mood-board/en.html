05 / START SOMETHING

Good things happen
when ideas meet.

Three possible combinations. Each is a conversation starter, with room for Scarlet’s own shapes and drawing.

WARM / TACTILE / EVERYDAY

The pocket neighbourhood

Warm shopfronts, chunky planters and a round bean. Add one slow delivery route and a few neighbours with somewhere to be.

  • Minami Lane · A welcoming street
  • Garden Galaxy · Matte, touchable pieces
  • Garden Story · A soft little resident
  • Kenney Car Kit · An everyday delivery van

Sketch first: a home, a post office and one planted corner.

OPEN / USEFUL / INTIMATE

The living dollhouse

A town you can peek into. Objects tell you what a room does; the bean crosses over, touches something, and your note or letter opens.

  • Mega Mall Story · Rooms full of purpose
  • MakeRoom · A crafted interior
  • Kind Words 2 · Paper as a small ritual
  • Station to Station · An arrive-pause-depart rhythm

Sketch first: a desk, a letter tray and one clear walking path.

DRAWN / LIGHT / PLAYFUL

The paper daydream

Paper silhouettes on a quiet illustrated ground, with delicate little citizens. Keep the broad shapes calm and give one small thing a warm accent.

  • Paperholm · Simple folded architecture
  • Carto · Clean illustrated ground
  • Hidden Folks · A few lively pen strokes
  • Mini Motorways · Quiet little routes

Sketch first: three roof shapes and a bean at actual phone size.
